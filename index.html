The basics

Fracture-Bench brings neural surrogate models to phase-field fracture simulation. It compares operator-learning and generative architectures on shared data splits and evaluation metrics across two high-fidelity datasets. The playground shows their predictions next to the finite-element results, so the differences between architectures can be explored directly. You can find the project resources below:
